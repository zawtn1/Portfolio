01. About me

I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Louisville (Louisville, KY), where I also earned my M.S. with a 3.91 GPA. I have hands-on experience building multimodal deep learning systems, LLM pipelines, and scalable ML infrastructure.

My research spans RGB–infrared fusion for object detection, distributed model training, and LLM fine-tuning. I design attention-based architectures that let detectors like YOLOv5 see better by combining visible and thermal imagery. On the language side, I build retrieval-augmented generation systems and conversational agents with LangChain, vector databases, and local LLMs.

I care about doing ML properly: rigorous ablation studies, honest benchmarking, reproducible pipelines, and tested, well-documented code.
